## 📌 Project Overview

This project explored how extreme heat events may affect cancer survivors living in South Florida. The study examined perceptions of heat-related health risks, adaptation strategies, barriers to protection, and concerns regarding long-term health impacts.

The project was completed as part of graduate-level qualitative research training and focused on applying qualitative methods including interview design, coding, thematic analysis, and interpretation of findings.

---

## 🎯 Research Objectives

- Explore perceived impacts of extreme heat on cancer survivors
- Identify barriers to heat adaptation and protection
- Examine awareness of heat-related health risks
- Understand coping strategies used during heatwaves
- Generate recommendations for supporting vulnerable populations during extreme heat events

## 🔍 Key Themes Identified

### Theme 1: Physical Health Vulnerability

Participants described increased fatigue, dehydration concerns, and heat sensitivity during periods of extreme temperature.

### Theme 2: Access to Cooling Resources

Financial and transportation barriers influenced access to cooling centers and air-conditioned environments.

### Theme 3: Emotional and Psychological Stress

Participants reported anxiety regarding heat exposure and concerns about maintaining health during prolonged heat events.

### Theme 4: Adaptive Coping Strategies

Common strategies included:

- Limiting outdoor activity
- Increasing hydration
- Using air-conditioned environments
- Seeking support from family and community networks

### Theme 5: Need for Targeted Public Health Support

Participants emphasized the importance of heat-health education and support programs tailored to cancer survivors.

---

## 💡 Public Health Implications

The findings highlight the importance of:

- Heat-health education for medically vulnerable populations
- Improved access to cooling resources
- Community outreach during extreme weather events
- Climate adaptation planning for cancer survivors
- Integration of climate resilience into survivorship care
